Yes, AI agents can be used to replicate real-world trading environments in order to study how external influences shape stock market behavior. Factors such as macroeconomic conditions, regulatory and policy shifts, corporate fundamentals, and major global events consistently affect investor decisions and market dynamics. Understanding these elements is essential for improving investment strategies and optimizing returns.

To address this challenge, we introduce StockAgent, a multi-agent framework powered by large language models (LLMs). StockAgent is designed to emulate the behavior of investors interacting with real-world market conditions. Through this system, users can examine how different external variables influence trading decisions, behavioral patterns, and overall profitability. The platform enables detailed analysis of investor responses to market stimuli and the resulting price movements.

A key advantage of StockAgent is its ability to eliminate test-set leakage, a common issue in existing AI-driven trading simulations. The system ensures that LLM-based agents operate without access to prior knowledge related to the evaluation data, thereby maintaining experimental integrity and preventing biased outcomes.

Within the StockAgent framework, multiple LLMs are assessed in a simulated trading environment that closely mirrors actual market settings. Experimental findings highlight how major external factors affect investor behavior and stock price dynamics. By examining trading decisions in scenarios where agents have no prior exposure to relevant market data, this research sheds light on free trading behavior and emergent market patterns.

Overall, StockAgent offers a novel approach for analyzing the interaction between external forces and stock trading activities. The insights generated from these simulations contribute to the development of more reliable LLM-based investment advisory systems and stock recommendation tools.

Architecture

This project introduces StockAgent, a large language model (LLM)-driven multi-agent system designed to simulate realistic stock market environments and investor behavior. The framework recreates daily market operations, beginning with market and agent initialization, followed by a sequence of financial activities such as interest repayment, loan repayment, bankruptcy checks, financial event updates, and loan decision-making. Within each trading session, agents generate trading strategies, make buy and sell decisions, and respond dynamically to price updates, closely mimicking real-world market interactions. The system also incorporates a bulletin board (BBS) module where agents share trading tips, enabling information diffusion and behavioral influence among participants. Additionally, agents estimate their next-day actions, including buying, selling, or borrowing decisions, allowing the study of expectation formation and forward-looking behavior. A key feature of StockAgent is its prevention of test data leakage, ensuring that agents operate without prior knowledge of evaluation data, thereby maintaining experimental integrity. By modeling the impact of macroeconomic conditions, financial reports, policy changes, and global events, the project provides a controlled yet realistic environment to analyze trading behaviors, market dynamics, and profitability patterns, offering valuable insights for LLM-based investment research and intelligent trading systems.
